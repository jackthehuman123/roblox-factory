# Council criteria

Each item scores 0, 1 or 2. 1 is anything between the 0 and 2 lines. Any 0 means the brief needs a change before go.

## Hook (council-hook)

| Id | Criterion | 0 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Understood from title + thumbnail idea in 3 seconds | Needs a sentence of explanation before it makes sense | One picture and a few words say what you do |
| H2 | Fun within 60 seconds, no lore or long tutorial first | First minute is reading, waiting or a tutorial | Player is doing the fun thing within a minute of spawning |
| H3 | One twist on a loop players already know | Entirely new loop to learn, or a plain copy with no twist | A familiar loop with one clear, new twist |

## Loop (council-loop)

| Id | Criterion | 0 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Reason to return tomorrow | Nothing changes or waits for the player between sessions | Clear progress, streak or timed reward pulls them back next day |
| L2 | Content that can grow weekly | Adding content means new systems | New maps, items or modes slot in as data each week |
| L3 | Better with friends | Solo play is the same or better | Playing with friends is clearly more fun or more rewarding |
| L4 | Things to buy that aren't pay-to-win and don't need paid random items | Purchases are power or paid loot boxes, or nothing to buy | Cosmetics, convenience or access players want, none of it random or power |

## Feasibility (council-feasibility)

| Id | Criterion | 0 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Core buildable on the kit in ≤ 5 build sessions | Needs systems the kit lacks and many sessions | Core is kit modules + game glue, ≤ 5 sessions |
| F2 | Runs on a phone (touch controls, low part count) | Needs keyboard/mouse or a heavy world | Plays with touch; graybox stays small |
| F3 | Playtestable by 1–4 people | Needs a full server to work | Core works and can be judged with 1–4 players |

## Phase 2 testing note

Target day-1 return ≥ 15% and day-7 return ≥ 5% from small ad cohorts.
